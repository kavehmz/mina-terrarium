      Terrarium

      Mina lives here. Nothing about her day is scripted.

      Her brain ticks every second. TypeSafe's Jev is System One: it feels hunger, tiredness, boredom, loneliness, and notices when something is wrong. When a feeling crosses the line, Claude is System Two: it decides what she wants to do and hands her body a plan. Code walks, cooks, showers, sleeps.

      While she thinks, the house clock slows to real time, because thinking takes real seconds of a life.

      You see everything. Her brain sees only her body, her room, what she can hear, and her phone.

      The brain only runs while someone is watching. Roughly $1 an hour at 60×.
